# OG Camera: Skate 3's camera in skate.

## skate. side (all EBX data, bundle win32/levels/game/bam_levelroot/bam_levelroot + 5 level copies)
- `gameplay/cameras/rigs/camerarig_onboardfov` / `_offboardfov`: DingoFOVCameraControllerData, FOV 55 driven by
  variable `CameraFloatVariable_Onboard_FOV` (default 65, written from the FOV setting), FOV-to-distance scaling
  (ref 65, x1.25 above, x1.5 below). Rendered FOV is vertical (camera +0xac, as Hall of Meat uses it).
- `gameplay/cameras/framingsets/onboardlowcameraframing`: pivot (0, 0.85, 0); framings by pitch -45/0/+45:
  (x, y, z) = (-1.35, -0.1, 2), (-1.35, 0, 1.9), (-1.35, 0.2, 2). x is lateral and is multiplied by
  SideBiasFactor (variable default 0) and SkaterMirroredFactor; z is distance back; offsets are in the pitched frame.
- `onboardhighcameraframing`: pivot (0, 1, 0); one framing (-1, 0, 3) at pitch -12.
- High pitch comes from `camerafloatvariable_onboardhigh_defaultpitch` (-12), copied to `_onboard_high_unifiedpitch`
  by the director tree each update (not touched by the settings updater; the offboard one is).
- `dingoesmcameradirector_onboard_low/_high`: FollowCameraControllerData yaw/pitch damping 25.
- Fisheye exists: `camera/camerapresets/lens/ve_camlens_basic_fisheye` (FilmicEffects LensDistortion gain 0.413,
  cubic 1.053, stretch 0.296), used only by replay cameras through `camerarig_common_cameralensselector`.

## Skate 3 side (db/skatercollections.vlt, class layouts decoded, names brute-forced: src/../brute.c, s3cls.py)
- `camera_shots` (243 shots): PositionDistance / PositionElevation / PositionHeading, Framing{Yaw,Pitch,Roll,LensLength},
  Smoothing{Yaw,Pitch,Elevation,Direction}, TransitionTime. FramingLensLength = 12 for every shot.
- Low chase: 2 m, elevation 0, heading 180. chase_flat_slow 1.8 m / 7 deg / heading -160, framing yaw +-15..28,
  roll +-1; chase_flat_fast 2.5 m / 5 deg, roll +-7. Grind 2 m / 15 deg. air_big 4 m / 15 deg / heading -155.
- High chase: 3 m / 30 deg, framing pitch 3; high_chase2 4.4 m / 26.6 deg.
- camera.dynamics: HeadingSpring 400, HeadingDamping 100, HeadingSmoothing 0.87, MaxPitch 35, NearClip 0.11.
- camera.subject_tracker: spring 1500, damping 40. rig_config: ArmLength 1.7, YOffset 0.3.
- camera_tracker pan/tilt smoothing 0.5-0.6 / 0.3-0.85. camera_lag_profile push/pump: 3 s curves, max smoothing 0.8/0.88.
- camera_tether_behaviour initial_relative_orientation (-0.25, 1, -1.6).

## 1.0.0 mapping (edits.txt)
- FOV 80 fixed (12 mm on a 36 mm gate = 112 deg horizontal = 80 deg vertical at 16:9), scaling off, on and off board.
- Low framing z 2.0 (2.1 at +-45). High default pitch -27 (30 deg elevation, 3 deg aim-up).
- Follow yaw damping 25 -> 4, pitch 25 -> 6 (Skate 3 heading settles in ~0.25 s).

## 1.1.0 (edits_1.1.0.txt, generated by gen_edits.py; 66 bundles)
- Fisheye: the 25 gameplay "look" VEs (lighting/ve/camera/ve_look_*, halloween ones) override EnableLensDistortion = false
  at priority 2 over the base camera VE ve_dingo_50mm_neutral_base (gain 0.03). All set true; base gets skate.'s fisheye
  curve (gain 0.413, cubic 1.053, stretch 0.296) with CubicGain/Stretch added to PropertyOverrides and
  FieldFlagOverride0 bits 13/14 (bit n = nth field of FilmicEffectsComponentData).
- Shots: ESM tree states (dingoesmcameradirector_esmcameradirectortree, 170 states) each push a CameraFramingBlendData from
  their action graph (<id>_ag, copies in _ag_commandactiondata_*). LocalRotationOffset x = pitch (negative looks down),
  y = yaw. Skate 3 -> skate.: y = d sin(el), z = d cos(el), rotation x = -(el + framing pitch).
  ONCL_Grind 99b08e7 (rail 2 m/15), ONCL_Manual a60491d (close_manual 1.1 m/-5, up 13, yaw -15),
  ONCL_BigAir b076a5c (air_big 4 m/15, up 5), ONCH_Manual aea49a4 (z 2.25), ONCH_BigAir ba1b9e8 (z 5).
