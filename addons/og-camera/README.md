# OG Camera

Skate 3's camera in skate., built from Skate 3's own camera tables and matched against Skate 3 footage.

## What changes
- **Skate 3's lens.** Skate 3 shoots every gameplay shot on a 12 mm lens. That is about an 80 degree field
  of view, much wider than skate.'s, so you see more of the spot around you.
- **The fisheye.** The picture bends at the edges like Skate 3's filmer lens. skate. already has a
  fisheye lens for replays; this pack puts it on the gameplay camera, at every time of day.
- **Low camera = Skate 3's chase cam.** 2 m straight behind you, level and low, with you in the middle of
  the picture.
- **High camera = Skate 3's high chase cam.** 3 m out and looking down at about 27 degrees.
- **The swing.** Skate 3's camera takes about a quarter of a second to swing round behind you when you
  turn. skate.'s snaps round almost at once. This pack gives it Skate 3's lazy swing.
- **Skate 3's shots.** The camera changes shot by itself like Skate 3's:
  - **Grinds:** Skate 3's rail cam, raised 15 degrees and looking down at the board.
  - **Manuals:** Skate 3's low manual cam, close in and tilted up at you.
  - **Big air:** pulls back to 4 m (5 m on the high camera) and looks up at you from level with you.
- **Air and landings.** The camera follows you up less in the air, so the board rises above it, and landing
  on a slope no longer tips the camera down with the slope.
- Off the board (walking, climbing) gets the same lens.

Works on every map, including custom maps from the MODS page.

## Use
Install from the launcher's MODS page, or drop the folder into `Mods/`. Switch between skate.'s high and
low skating camera in its camera settings as usual; each one now plays like Skate 3's.

While this pack is on, the game's **Field of View** slider has no effect on the skating camera: the FOV
is Skate 3's. Turn the pack off in the MODS tab of the ReSkate menu (Insert) to get skate.'s camera back.

When skate. updates, this pack needs rebuilding for the new version (ReSkate shows it as outdated until then).

## Requirements
- ReSkate.
- No DLL: this is a data pack only. It is separate from Hall of Meat; use either or both.
