# Hall of Meat

Skate 3's Hall of Meat, back in skate. through ReSkate. Crash, and the picture closes in to a dark,
grainy spotlight while your skeleton shows through the body, every bruised, dislocated and broken
bone lit up as it happens. The Thrasher score panel counts your bail the way Skate 3 did.

## What's in it
- **X-ray skeleton** drawn with Skate 3's own bone meshes and texture, on the ragdoll from the moment
  you crash until you get back up.
- **Skate 3's damage model**: 25 body parts, each with up to six damage levels from bruised to
  broken, with Skate 3's points and impact thresholds, and hits that spread to connected parts.
- **Skate 3's score graphs** for bail time, drop, air, speed and rotation, and its HUD rules for
  when each row appears.
- **Skate 3's bone-crunch sounds**, one for every new injury.
- **The HoM look**: dark cool grade, spotlight, film grain and a red flash on a break.
- Triggers on every crash, on or off the board. Glides, hippy jumps and plants don't count.

## Requirements
- **ReSkate with Hall of Meat built in.** This package holds the art, meshes and sounds. The code that
  draws them is part of ReSkate.dll. Until it ships in an official ReSkate release, use the build from
  https://github.com/layton-brewer/ReSkate-Skate3-Hall-Of-Meat/releases/latest
- Offline play.

## Use
Install from the launcher's MODS page, or drop the folder into `Mods/`. Turn it off in the MODS tab
of the ReSkate menu (Insert) to switch Hall of Meat off.

Console (`~`): `hom status` shows your best bail.
